Passive MIDI SysEx capture

Enable MIDI, choose the input for each DIN pair, and arm the capture. Start the dump from your instrument, then stop and save the raw .syx file. If names are unclear, send a small dump from one instrument at a time to confirm the route.

Files contain complete SysEx messages in receive order. Check them against the expected dump, byte count, and checksum. This page sends no MIDI and does not measure DIN timing.
